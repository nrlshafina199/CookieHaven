🍪 Privacy Policy

At CookieHaven, we value your trust. This policy explains how we handle your data.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect information necessary to fulfill your cookie orders, including your Name, Delivery Address, and Phone Number.

2. Data Usage

Your data is used solely for processing your order and arranging delivery. We do not sell your data to third parties.

3. Cookie Policy

We use a strictly necessary session cookie (AUTH_SESSION) to keep you logged into your account. This cookie is essential for the website to function. We also use Local Storage to remember if you have dismissed our cookie consent banner.

4. PDPA Compliance

We comply with the Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA) to ensure your personal information is kept secure and handled responsibly.
